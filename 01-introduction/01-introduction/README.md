# Section 1: Introduction

## Section Overview

This section introduces the core role of Product Management and the difference between delivering software and creating successful products. It provides the foundation for later sections on strategy, discovery, design, development, and measurement.

## Learning Goals

After completing this section, the learner should understand:

- The responsibilities of a Product Manager
- How digital products may contain several smaller product areas
- Why Agile delivery alone does not create an Agile product organization
- Why early validation and outcome-oriented planning reduce product risk
