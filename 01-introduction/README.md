# Part 1: Introduction

## Part Overview

This part introduces the Product Manager role, the nature of digital products, and the operating principles that distinguish effective product work from feature delivery. It establishes the language and mental models used throughout the rest of the course.

## Part Learning Goals

After completing this part, the learner should understand:

- What a digital product is and how it may be decomposed across teams
- What Product Managers own and how they collaborate with design and engineering
- Why product success requires discovery as well as delivery
- Why traditional feature-driven processes create avoidable product risk
