# Part 1: Introduction

## Part Overview

This part introduces the foundational concepts of Product Management, the digital product, and the product operating model. It covers how product teams are structured, how the PM role varies across domains, and how the core product triad (Product Manager, Product Designer, Tech Lead) collaborates to address the four key product risks. By completing this part, the learner will understand what it means to operate as an outcome-oriented, empowered product team.

## Part Learning Goals

After completing this part, the learner should understand:
- The fundamental responsibilities of the Product Manager, Product Designer, and Tech Lead roles.
- The differences between a product delivery team (shipping output) and an empowered product team (solving business problems).
- How the PM role shifts depending on business model, domain, and data/technical requirements.
- The concept of product-market fit and the importance of validation.
