# Scenario: Transcript to Mental Model

## Prompt

The user supplies complete metadata and a repetitive transcript that moves back and forth between outputs, outcomes, feature commitments, and team autonomy.

## Expected Without Skill

The agent may follow transcript chronology, preserve repetition as separate sections, or produce a broad summary without one usable mental model.

## Required Behavior With Skill

- Define one observable learning outcome for the lecture.
- Identify the instructor's central claim and minimum supporting claims.
- Reorganize the material around one accurate mental model rather than transcript order.
- Reflect the learning outcome in the Lecture Overview without adding a mandatory template section.
- Remove repeated or tangential material that does not support the learning outcome.
- Keep all lecture-content claims grounded in the supplied transcript.

## Pass Condition

The guide has one clear learning outcome and coherent mental model, can be understood without the transcript, and does not introduce unsupported teaching content.
