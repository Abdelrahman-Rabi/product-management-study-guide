Your goal

Acknowledge strategic timeframes, identify emerging password-replacement tech, and diagnose 5-year growth trajectories for tech-driven B2B/B2C markets.

Exercise overview

This exercise guides you through diagnosing a product's market trajectory (Growing, Shrinking, or Stable) over a five-year timeframe. Five years is the standard strategic horizon for software product organizations planning their product vision and long-term roadmap.

Part A: Warm-Up Market Evaluations

Analyze how password managers, dentist scheduling software, desktop antivirus, and secondhand car marketplaces perform under structural shifts in security, demographics, device form factors, and mature sales saturation.

Part B: The Uber Ride Hailing Case Study

The ride-hailing industry is set on a Growing trajectory over a 5-year strategic horizon. Growth is driven by urbanization and smartphone/connectivity prevalence, while limited by local regulatory shutdowns.

Part C: Market Trajectory Worksheet

Audit your own product's market trajectory over a 5-year strategic timeframe. If your market is stable or shrinking, pivot your product strategy toward adjacent growing domains (e.g. desktop antivirus shifting to cloud network security) to protect business viability.

Common Misunderstandings

"Biometric security is the perfect replacement for passwords"

Correction: While biometrics remove login friction, they introduce a major vulnerability: permanence. If database hackers leak your cryptographic biometric prints, you cannot reset your face or fingerprint. Modern security must combine biometrics with multi-factor encryption layers.

"Market size is the same as market growth"

Correction: A market can be massive but shrinking (e.g. desktop PC antivirus). Massive shrinking markets are highly competitive and undergo price wars. A growing market can be small today but offers high margins and a stream of new buyers who are easy to acquire.

Key concepts and definitions

  • Zero Login: Authentication utilizing passive behavioral metrics (typing rhythm, location) to log in without user action.
  • Biometric Permanence: The security risk where physical characteristics (fingerprints, iris) cannot be reset if compromised.
  • Market Trajectory: The direction of a market's value, prevalence, and volume over a strategic timeframe (Grow, Shrink, Stable).
  • Form Factor Shift: Changes in the dominant hardware devices used by consumers (e.g. from desktop towers to smartphones).

Interactive Practice

Warm-Up Trajectory Matcher

Match each warm-up market to its estimated 5-year trajectory.

Password Managers

Incorrect. While security needs are increasing, passwords themselves are highly insecure and will be phased out by biometric standards.
Correct! The password manager market is shrinking. Authentication is shifting to passwordless Zero Logins and iris/fingerprint biometrics.
Incorrect. The transition to biometric standards will disrupt traditional passwords, shrinking this specific market.

Dentist Appointment Scheduling

Correct! Dentistry demands high motor dexterity that is difficult to automate with robotics or ML in 5 years, while an aging population increases patient volume.
Incorrect. Medical dexterity is extremely hard to automate with robots, and physical cleanings cannot be virtualized.
Incorrect. Demographics (aging populations) are driving steady expansion.

Authentication Tech Vulnerabilities

Explore the security loopholes in emerging authentication technologies.

Biometrics Scans (Iris, Face, Fingers)

Loopholes: Permanence. Unlike a text password, if your iris scan dataset is stolen, you cannot reset your biological eye structure. A compromised print is permanently compromised.

Zero Login (Behavioral tracking)

Loopholes: High variability. If a user breaks their typing hand, relocates, or changes occupations, the system's behavioral telemetry fails, locking out the user.

Implanted Microchips

Loopholes: Ethical barriers and social friction. Getting public adoption for subdermal microchips faces heavy resistance and privacy regulation hurdles.
